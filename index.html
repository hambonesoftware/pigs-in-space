HOW TO PLAY

Choose any of the three missions. You start with five shields shared across your arcade run. Finish a round to earn points and unlock a tougher round.

🐔 CHICKENVADERSMove left/right. Fire sausages at space chickens; dodge their eggs. Clear the fleet to win.
🍝 PASTAROIDSMove in all four directions toward the moving ship at the top. Pasta knocks you back. Fire to karate-chop nearby meatballs.
🛸 GONZOID ESCAPEFly through the canyon without touching the walls. Dodge pizzas and fire curved Boomerays at robots. Survive to the exit.

Phone / tablet: on-screen D-pad and fire button. Desktop: arrow keys or WASD, Space to fire, P/Esc to pause. You can also steer by dragging on the game field.
